The generator constructs the monocoque, nose, sidepods, engine cover, floor,
diffuser, wings, mirrors, and material lanes from Blender primitives plus
project-authored numeric vertex profiles. Godot adds original wheels, brake
hardware, suspension, halo, driver, steering wheel, dashboard, and animation.
The user-supplied Formula-car photograph and recorded-game frames were used only
as high-level visual references for generic open-wheel proportions, daylight
readability, and cockpit framing. No model geometry, texture, branding, sponsor
mark, team identity, or exact livery was extracted or copied.
