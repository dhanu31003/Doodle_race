# RaceGlyph Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

RaceGlyph (`com.raceglyph.game`) is published by Neutale. This policy explains how the Android version of RaceGlyph handles information.

## Information stored on your device

RaceGlyph stores game settings, selected vehicles and colorways, saved custom tracks and thumbnails, best laps, race progress, and race results in the app's private local storage. This information is not sent to Neutale. It remains on your device until you delete it in RaceGlyph, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup and retain-on-uninstall are disabled for RaceGlyph.

The Settings screen lets you export a copy of supported local game data, reset race progress, or delete all local RaceGlyph data.

## Nearby multiplayer

RaceGlyph uses Google Play services Nearby Connections to let people in the same physical area discover one another and play private device-to-device races. When you choose Nearby multiplayer, Android may ask for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Nearby devices, and, on some Android versions, location-related permissions. RaceGlyph uses these permissions only to discover and connect to nearby RaceGlyph players. RaceGlyph does not use them to store or infer your precise location.

During a nearby room, RaceGlyph exchanges transient game information with the connected devices, including a room code, participant names chosen in the app, room membership, readiness, circuit data, race inputs, race state, and results. This information is used to run the private session and is not sent to a Neutale game server. Session information is discarded when the room ends, except for results or progress that the app stores locally on each participant's device.

Google states that the Nearby Connections SDK collects usage analytics, including performance metrics such as discovery and connection latency, reliability, and throughput, and device information such as device model, country, build version, and application package name. Google uses this information to improve the Nearby experience, and collection is controlled through the device's Google Usage & diagnostics setting. Google's handling of that information is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Information RaceGlyph does not collect

RaceGlyph does not require an account and does not include advertising, targeted advertising, marketing analytics, crash reporting, chat, contact-list access, photo or media access, or microphone access. Neutale does not sell or share RaceGlyph player information for advertising.

## Children

RaceGlyph is not directed to children under 13. Its intended audience is people aged 13 and older.

## Security and retention

RaceGlyph minimizes information handling by keeping game data in Android's app-private storage and using encrypted Nearby Connections for device-to-device sessions. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Local information remains until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Nearby session information is retained only for the session as described above.

## Changes to this policy

If RaceGlyph's information practices change, Neutale will update this policy and its effective date before the changed version is released where required.
